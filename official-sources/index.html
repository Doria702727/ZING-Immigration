Official Sources for Canadian Immigration Updates

A curated reference board for official immigration sources, policy updates, public data, and practical tools we monitor in our daily immigration practice.
Curated by Doria Shi, RCIC · ZING Immigration Consultancy Inc.

这个页面的用途

加拿大移民信息更新频繁，不同渠道的解读也很多。对于申请人来说，并不需要读懂每一份政策文件，但需要知道重要信息应该从哪里核实。

这个信息入口的目的，是把常用的一手信息和官方来源集中在一起，作为申请人查询、核对和理解政策变化的参考入口。它也反映了我作为 RCIC 在日常工作中如何持续关注官方来源、政策更新和公开数据。

如何使用

可以从 新闻动态 查看官方新闻和公告；通过 政策更新 查看政策指引、法律法规和未来监管方向；使用 实用工具 查询处理时间、费用、表格、NOC、工资、DLI、PGWP 专业方向和 ATIP 等信息。

这个页面不替代个案分析，也不要求申请人自行解读所有政策细节。它的作用是帮助大家回到官方来源，先确认信息出处，再结合自己的情况进一步判断。

新闻动态

官方新闻、公告和公共讨论方向。
官方公告IRCC 新闻中心

IRCC 正式发布的新闻、项目公告和部长声明。用于确认移民政策、项目开放、项目调整或重大方向变化是否已经由官方发布。

操作通知IRCC 通知

劳动力政策ESDC 新闻

加拿大就业、劳动力、技能培训、行业短缺及 LMIA 相关政策更新。适合观察哪些行业正在被政府持续关注，以及临时外劳和雇主合规方向是否出现变化。

公共讨论国会 CIMM 委员会会议

国会委员会，尤其是 CIMM 委员会，当下热议的移民议题。可以从中看到公共讨论的方向和未来可能的政策导向。

政策指引、法规和未来政策方向。

IRCC 对审理指引的更新。用于查看某个项目是否出现新的审理重点、材料要求或执行逻辑变化。

审理指引IRCC 审理指引

IRCC 如何解释和执行某类申请要求。适合用于理解规则在实际审理中可能如何被适用。

法律来源IRPA

加拿大移民条例原文。用于进一步确认具体监管要求、豁免条件和申请规则。

政策来源公共政策

临时公共政策、特殊措施和政策协议的入口。用于查看某些临时措施是否仍然有效，以及其适用范围是否明确。

法规计划IRCC 法规变化计划

IRCC 未来可能推进的法规调整。它不是已经生效的申请规则，但适合观察政策未来可能的调整方向。

Why This Board Exists

Immigration information is everywhere, but not every source carries the same weight. For applicants making decisions about study, work, permanent residence, or status planning, the first challenge is often knowing where reliable information actually comes from.

This board is ZING's curated starting point for official Canadian immigration sources. It brings together the government pages, policy instructions, legislation, public data, and research tools we monitor in practice, so applicants can trace an update back to its original source.

It does not replace case-specific advice, and applicants are not expected to read every page in full. It is here to help applicants begin with trusted sources, ask better questions, and make decisions with clearer information.

01

News

Official announcements, public notices, and policy discussion signals.
Official AnnouncementIRCC Newsroom

Official IRCC news releases, program announcements, and ministerial statements. This is a useful place to confirm whether a major immigration update has been formally announced by the government.

Operational NoticeIRCC Notices

Operational updates from IRCC, including fee changes, temporary measures, and program-specific notices. Useful for checking effective dates, who is affected, and how a change is being implemented.

Labour PolicyESDC News

Federal updates on employment, labour market needs, skills training, sector shortages, and LMIA-related policy. This helps track how workforce priorities and employer compliance issues may be evolving.

Public DebateParliament CIMM Meetings

Current immigration topics being studied by parliamentary committees, especially CIMM. This can help show where public debate is focused and where future policy pressure may be building.

02

Policy Updates

Program guidance, legislation, public policies, and regulatory planning.
Program GuidanceIRCC Program Delivery Updates

Updates to IRCC program delivery guidance. This is often where changes in processing focus, document expectations, or operational interpretation first become visible.

Program GuidanceIRCC Program Delivery Instructions

IRCC guidance on how different programs and requirements are applied in practice. It is a useful reference point for understanding how a rule may be assessed beyond the basic checklist.

Legal SourceIRPA

The Immigration and Refugee Protection Act. This is one of the legal foundations for key concepts such as status, study authorization, work authorization, and inadmissibility.

Legal SourceIRPR

The Immigration and Refugee Protection Regulations. Useful for checking specific regulatory requirements, exemptions, and application rules.

Policy SourceMinisterial Instructions

Formal instructions that support many selection mechanisms, including Express Entry rounds and category-based selection. Useful for tracing certain invitation rules back to their official authority.

Policy SourcePublic Policies

Temporary public policies, special measures, and policy agreements. This is where applicants can check whether a temporary measure is still active and who it applies to.

Regulatory PlanIRCC Forward Regulatory Plan

Planned or possible regulatory changes from IRCC. These are not application rules already in force, but they can be helpful for understanding where future policy changes may be headed.

03

Tools

Practical lookup tools and ZING-built reference resources.
ZING ToolZING High-Wage Occupation Dashboard

A ZING-built reference tool for looking at high-wage occupations through wage data, NOC classification, and immigration planning. It is intended as a planning reference, not as an official selection rule.

Lookup ToolIRCC Processing Times

IRCC estimated processing times by application type. These are average reference times and can help with planning for extensions, travel, work, study, and status transitions.

Lookup ToolIRCC Fee List

Current IRCC application fees for temporary residence, permanent residence, citizenship, the right of permanent residence fee, and other applications. Best checked again before submission, especially around fee update periods.

Application FormsIRCC Forms and Guides

Official IRCC forms and application guides. This helps applicants confirm they are using the current version rather than an outdated form or a third-party copy.

Lookup ToolCRS Calculator

A tool for estimating Express Entry CRS scores. It helps applicants see how factors such as language, education, spouse profile, Canadian experience, and provincial nomination may affect their ranking.

Occupation ToolNOC / TEER Search

Canada's official occupation classification system. It is used to check NOC codes and TEER levels, but the analysis should be based on main duties and actual work performed, not job title alone.

Wage DataJob Bank Wage Search

Wage information by occupation and region in Canada. This can support job offer review, LMIA wage analysis, high-wage or low-wage positioning, and broader labour market planning.

Study ToolDLI List

The official list of designated learning institutions. It can help confirm whether a school is a DLI and whether it has programs that may be PGWP-eligible at the institution level.

Study ToolPGWP Field of Study / CIP Code

IRCC's field of study and CIP code reference for PGWP eligibility. This is especially relevant for college-level programs where the program's field of study may affect post-graduation work permit eligibility.

Public RecordsIRCC Completed ATIP Requests

Completed Access to Information request summaries involving IRCC. This can surface policy records, data references, internal documents, or research leads beyond standard public webpages.
